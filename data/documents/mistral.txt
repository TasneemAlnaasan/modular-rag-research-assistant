Mistral AI is a Paris-based open-weight AI company founded in 2023 by former Meta AI and Google DeepMind researchers Arthur Mensch, Guillaume Lample, and Timothée Lacroix. Its major products include open-weights models like Mistral 7B, Mixtral 8x7B, Mistral Large, and the conversational assistant Le Chat. The company's vision centers on open science, European technological sovereignty, and providing businesses with customizable, energy-efficient AI models.
