OpenAI is an AI research and deployment company founded in San Francisco in 2015 by Sam Altman, Elon Musk, Greg Brockman, and others as a non-profit lab aiming to ensure Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) benefits all of humanity, later restructuring into a capped-profit subsidiary in 2019 and converting to a public benefit corporation in 2025. Its flagship products include the GPT model series, ChatGPT, DALL-E, Sora, and Whisper. The company's core vision revolves around developing safe, highly capable AGI and distributing its benefits broadly and equitably.
