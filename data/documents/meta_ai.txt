Meta AI is the dedicated artificial intelligence division of Meta Platforms, originating from the establishment of FAIR (Facebook AI Research) in 2013 led by Yann LeCun to advance foundational machine learning. Its key contributions include the open-weights Llama model series, the Meta AI assistant, and the creation of PyTorch. Meta AI's vision is focused on advancing open science, democratizing AI access, and seamlessly integrating intelligent agents across everyday social applications.
