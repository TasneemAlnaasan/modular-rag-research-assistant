Hugging Face is an open platform and global community hub for machine learning and open-source AI, founded in 2016 by Clément Delangue, Julien Chaumond, and Thomas Wolf as a conversational app before pivoting into developer infrastructure. Its main products include the Hugging Face Hub, the Transformers library, Datasets, Spaces, and Gradio. The platform's philosophy is to democratize machine learning by enabling open collaboration, model sharing, and accessible deployment for developers worldwide.
