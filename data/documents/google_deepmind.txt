Google DeepMind is Google's primary AI research division, formed in 2023 by combining DeepMind and Google Brain, tracing back to DeepMind's original founding in London in 2010 by Demis Hassabis, Shane Legg, and Mustafa Suleyman. Its premier products include the Gemini model suite, AlphaFold for protein structure prediction, Veo, and Imagen. The organization's vision is centered on solving fundamental intelligence to advance science and benefit humanity.
